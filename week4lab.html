Fishers, Indiana

Fishers is a city near central Indiana. It has great schools and lots of stores. Overall, it is a very nice place to live, and more people are moving here all the time.

Public Library

Fishers has a great public library in the downtown area. The Hamilton Southeastern public library has many amazing books available for checkout.

Farmers' Market

Fishers has a thriving farmers' market that happens in the downtown area every Saturday morning throughout the summer. It is a fun place to walk around and explore.

Handel's Ice Cream

Handel's Ice Cream is very refreshing on a hot summer day. They offer a wide variety of interesting flavors, so there is something for everyone to try.

My Hometown

I have a great personal connection to Fishers because I have lived here for most of my life. Almost everybody that I know lives here, and I have been going to public school here for as long as I have been in school. No matter where I go in the future, Fishers will always be important to me because it is the place I called home for the majority of my childhood.
